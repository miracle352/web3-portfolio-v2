03 / Approach

Good communities
are designed, then
look after themselves.

  1. 01

    Listen before building

    Every project has a different tempo. I start by learning the product, the people, and the friction already in the room.

  2. 02

    Make participation easy

    Clear paths, useful rituals, and messages people can act on are more valuable than a constant stream of activity.

  3. 03

    Keep the signal visible

    We track what matters, report plainly, and adjust the work around the community rather than forcing the community to fit a spreadsheet.
